<p><b>For Schafkopf evenings at the pub.</b> After every game one player records what was played, and the app works out to the cent who receives or pays how much. The app is in German.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Game types:</b> Rufspiel, Über Kreuz, Farbsolo, Wenz and Geier, with Laufende, Schneider, Schwarz, Tout and Sie, Kontra and Retour.</li>
<li><b>Doubling:</b> every player who knocked doubles the value (matchbox symbol); “6 Nixer” start a Bock round in which games count double.</li>
<li><b>Evening:</b> running totals with places, dealer and Bock counter; every game can be edited or deleted later and everything is recalculated at once.</li>
<li><b>Configurable rules:</b> tariffs, Laufende, Bock round, pot split for solo games; every evening keeps its own rules.</li>
<li><b>Closing and statistics:</b> final standings and who pays whom; statistics for one evening or all evenings with a balance history chart, game types, a knocking index, Bock king, solo specialist and favourite partners.</li>
<li><b>Sharing:</b> closing and statistics as text or as an image, for example to Signal or Telegram.</li>
<li><b>Backup:</b> save and restore a file (also exchangeable with the iPhone web app) and, if you like, automatically after every change, directly to Google Drive or into a file you pick once.</li>
<li><b>Evening commentator:</b> a funny, slightly teasing summary of the evening, or a review of all evenings, in Bavarian, from text templates or, if you like, written by a language model (Google Gemini with your own key).</li>
<li><b>Made for dimly lit pubs:</b> dark design, large buttons, works with a screen reader.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy:</b> everything stays on the device. The app uses the internet only for two optional extras: backup to Google Drive and the evening commentator with a language model (sends only after consent, without real names by default). No own server, no ads, no tracking.</p>

<p><b>Test version:</b> the watch app comes later. For the iPhone there is a web app with the same calculations.</p>
